Know Before You Go

Food to try

  • Borshch: beetroot soup, served with sour cream and garlic bread (pampushky).
  • Varenyky: dumplings with potato, cherry or cheese.
  • Chicken Kyiv: a buttery, breaded cutlet.
  • Banosh: Carpathian cornmeal with bryndza cheese.
  • Syrnyky: sweet fried cheese pancakes.

Useful phrases

  • Привіт (pryvit): Hello
  • Дякую (diakuiu): Thank you
  • Будь ласка (bud’ laska): Please / You’re welcome
  • Так / Ні (tak / ni): Yes / No
  • Скільки коштує? (skilky koshtuie?): How much is it?
  • Смачного (smachnoho): Enjoy your meal

Customs and etiquette

  • Greet people with a handshake and a smile; Ukrainians value politeness.
  • If invited home, take a small gift and expect to remove your shoes.
  • Give flowers in odd numbers; even numbers are for funerals.
  • Dress modestly in churches and monasteries.
  • Say “Ukraine”, not “the Ukraine”.

Getting around

  • Intercity trains are a popular, comfortable option; check timetables and book ahead.
  • Plan extra time between cities, as schedules can change.
  • City transport usually includes metro, trams, buses and ride-hailing apps.
  • Check opening hours for museums, churches and parks before you go; they vary by season and day.
